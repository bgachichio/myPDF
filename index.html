Edit any PDF. It never leaves your device.

myPDF is a free, open-source PDF editor that runs in your browser and works offline. Your file stays on your phone or laptop, because there is no server to upload it to.

  • Reorder, rotate, merge, split and extract pages
  • Edit text, highlight, draw and add notes
  • Fill and flatten forms, and sign
  • Redact for real, with the text removed and checked
  • Compress, add a password, and read scanned pages with OCR

Made by Brian Gachichio. Source code: github.com/bgachichio/myPDF (AGPL-3.0-or-later).
